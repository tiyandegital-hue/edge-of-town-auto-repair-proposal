How it works

No surprises is the whole process.

Mechanic anxiety comes from not knowing. This shop runs on a simple rule: you know the number before the work starts.

Step 01

Describe the problem

Call 902-543-5057 or send the estimate form. Noises, smells, warning lights — plain language is fine.

Step 02

Approve the estimate

The shop looks, quotes, and waits. Nothing is touched until you say yes to the number.

Step 03

Drive away

You pay for what was agreed — the work on the order, at the price on the order.
