Concept

The daily driver: the car that takes you to Bridgewater, the school run, the grocery run. Repairs and maintenance handled the same way every time — looked at, quoted, approved, then fixed.

  • Diagnosis first. The problem is found before any number is quoted — you hear what's wrong in plain language.
  • Estimate you approve. The price is agreed before work starts. If something else turns up mid-job, you hear about it before it costs you.
  • Maintenance that matters. The routine work that keeps a car out of the bay — oil, brakes, tires, belts — on a schedule that fits how you drive.

Concept

A truck that earns its keep can't sit in a bay for a week. Truck work here is scheduled around the job the truck has to do — diagnosed straight, quoted straight, back on Trunk 3.

  • Work-truck priority thinking. Downtime is the real cost; scheduling reflects that.
  • Straight quotes. Parts and labour broken out so you can see where the money goes.
  • Tow-ready checks. The systems that matter when the truck is loaded — brakes, cooling, drivetrain — checked with load in mind.

Concept

Utility and boat trailers, campers, and small cargo trailers. The work nobody thinks about until towing season — hitches, wiring, bearings, brakes.

  • Safety-critical first. Hitch, coupler, chains, lights and brakes get checked before anything cosmetic.
  • Bearing and brake service. The two failures that strand trailers on the highway — serviced before they fail.
  • Pre-season checkovers. One visit before the season starts beats a roadside repair in July.
