Privacy policy · concept draft

Privacy,
in plain language.

This is a concept draft written for a small Canadian business and aligned with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). The shop should have it reviewed by a lawyer before the site goes live.

What this site collects

On this concept site: nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and the estimate form does not send your details anywhere — the demo notice on the form says so plainly.

What the live site would collect

  • Estimate requests: your name, phone number, vehicle type and problem description — only what the shop needs to call you back with a quote.
  • Phone and email contact: if you call or email the shop, that conversation is between you and the shop.

Why it's collected

One reason only: to respond to your estimate or booking request. Your details are never sold, never shared with marketers, and never used for anything you didn't ask for.

How long it's kept

Estimate details are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting work, then deleted on request. Ask anytime: mailman2008@eastlink.ca.

Third parties

This concept site loads web fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). No map, video or social embed is used, and no analytics runs. The live site will list every third-party service here before launch.

Your rights

Under PIPEDA you can ask what personal information the shop holds about you, ask for corrections, and withdraw consent. Write to mailman2008@eastlink.ca — the shop aims to answer within 30 days.

Questions

Call 902-543-5057 or email mailman2008@eastlink.ca.
