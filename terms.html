This site

This is a concept proposal website built by Tiyan Digital to show what the shop's website could be. It is not the shop's official website. Content marked CONCEPT or SAMPLE is placeholder.

Estimates, not promises

Any estimate described on this site is an illustration of the shop's process. A real quote comes only from the shop — by phone, email or in person — after seeing the vehicle. Nothing on this site is a binding price.

The estimate form

On this concept site the form is simulated and sends nothing. On the live site, submitting an estimate request asks the shop to contact you back; it does not create a booking or a contract until the shop confirms.

Content

Photos on this site are generated concept images, labeled where shown. Text is original. The business name, address, phone and email are the shop's real public contact details, used here to demonstrate the proposal.

Liability

This concept site is provided as-is for demonstration. Vehicle advice on the live site will be general information, not a substitute for an in-person diagnosis.
